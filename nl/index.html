Alles in een platform

Alle onderdelen van het lasdossier blijven verbonden.

Projecten, lassen, inspecties, WPS/WPQ, bewijs, traceerbaarheid en rapportage werken vanuit dezelfde context.

01

Projects

Organise project scope, teams, inspection requirements and handover context before work starts.

02

Welds

Use a structured weld list as the anchor for inspection status, evidence and documentation.

03

Inspections

Record visual checks, findings, open points, photos and review status in one workflow.

04

WPS / WPQ

Keep procedure and qualification references visible alongside weld and project records.

05

Documents

Link drawings, certificates, reports and evidence to the right project context.

06

CE dossier

Prepare a clear dossier overview while the project is active, not at the end.

07

Traceability

Connect material context, heat numbers, welds, certificates and supporting records.

08

Reporting

Turn structured inspection records into clearer review and handover conversations.
