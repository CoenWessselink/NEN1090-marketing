Workflow detail

Connect certificates and heat numbers to project evidence

Material evidence is easier to review when it is attached to the project and weld context instead of stored as separate PDFs without workflow status.

  • Certificates and material references are linked to project context.
  • Heat numbers can remain visible in traceability evidence.
  • Inspection and material evidence can be reviewed together.
  • Reports can support clearer handover conversations.

FAQ

Common questions about EN 10204 material traceability documentation workflows

Does WeldInspect Pro replace EN 10204 material traceability?

No. WeldInspect Pro supports documentation workflows around EN 10204 material traceability. Official standard texts, certification and formal conformity decisions remain leading.

Which records can be connected to EN 10204 material traceability work?

Teams can connect projects, welds, inspection results, WPS/WPQ references, material certificates, photos, documents, open actions and report output.

Is this suitable for steel construction QA/QC teams?

Yes. The workflow is designed for steel construction, welding coordination, inspection coordination and project documentation teams that need clearer evidence and handover control.
